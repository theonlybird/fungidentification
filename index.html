How to play

You can choose a quiz of 10, 20 or 30 multiple choice questions. There will be 2–4 photos of a species in each round (taken from a single iNaturalist observation). 10 points per correct answer, 20 on Scientific Mode.

If the photos are too poor in quality, you can press New photos to load another observation of the same species. Per 10 questions, Beginners get 3 New photos uses, Novices get 2 and Intermediates/Experts get 1.

iNaturalist is a wonderful citizen-science website, but not all observations are guaranteed to be correct. Smell, feel and habitat of fungi are key to a successful ID, which you cannot do through a photo alone. This quiz is intended to be a fun learning tool - certainly do not eat anything based on this quiz. Try Teaching Mode to learn more.

You can review all of your answers and the observations at the end. Any mushrooms you get wrong are saved, so you can practise them later until you get each one right. Good luck!
